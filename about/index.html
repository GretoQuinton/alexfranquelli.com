Alex
Franquelli

Writer, critic and occasional political correspondent.

Alex Franquelli is an Italian writer based between Rome and London. His published work spans music criticism, live reporting, culture and politics, with writing in both English and Italian.

His archive includes work for The Line of Best Fit, PopMatters, All About Jazz, The Quietus, openDemocracy, OndaRock, IndieForBunnies and other publications.

He is currently completing his first novel.
