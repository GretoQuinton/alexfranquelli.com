Alex
Franquelli

Writer, novelist and film & TV professional.

I am an Italian writer based between Rome and London. I write in English and Italian about music, politics and culture, I am finishing my first novel, Greto, and my professional career is in film and television.

My published archive includes work for The Line of Best Fit, PopMatters, All About Jazz, The Quietus, openDemocracy, OndaRock, IndieForBunnies and other publications. I am rebuilding that archive here, including pieces that have disappeared from their original homes.

The Film & TV section covers my more recent professional work and will grow as projects can be shown publicly.
