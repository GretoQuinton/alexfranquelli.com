Interview with Kristoffer Rygg (Ulver)

18/04/2011

Can you tell me what happened with the album’s title? Why has it changed? I know you said you guys have a thing with royalty, but wasn’t there a concept behind the link between places and music that you wanted to explore with this album?

This is an extreme album in the broadest sense: it's got great and deep psychedelic improvised tracks, ballads, but also an intense use of upbeat drums like never before in the last 10 years. How much is due to a natural maturation and how much is because of Daniel’s influence?

How was it working with Daniel? I can imagine it is not always easy to try and restrain someone with such a huge creativity.

How strong is the influence of a project such as Aethenor in an album like this? It seems to me that you’ve let improvisation guide you through the tracks.

The improvisation we have just spoken about is countered by a very strong “pop” feel. And when I say “pop”, I mean good pop music (even some of Miles Davis’ pieces can be defined as “pop”). It seems to me that this is the first time you venture into pop music in such an open and straight way. What is the reason behind this? Is it another of the challenges the band has taken during the years or did it come out spontaneously?

Talking about pop, how did the collaboration with pop singer Siri Stranger come along? Her background is so different it’s amazing how it fits your music perfectly.

In your previous albums, Ulver have been inspired by composers such as Wagner. Who do you think is the influence behind the new album?

After choosing William Blake for one of your albums, I was amazed by the choice of including a poem from Keith Waldrop in ‘Stone Angel’. Whose idea was it?

How was it working with John Fryer? Was he easy to work with?

I was reading a few reviews of the album and I think it’s funny how prog websites and magazines depict it as a “prog” work, metal websites can hear some metal in there, indie websites and magazines can hear the influence of more than an indie figure.

After all these years, do you think that you have reached a “Ulver sound”, so to speak ?

Is it true that there is a dvd coming out ?

If Ulver were to dissolve tomorrow, what would be your everlasting memory?
