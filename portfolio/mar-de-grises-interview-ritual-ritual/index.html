Interview for MAR DE GRISES by Alex Franquelli PLEASE, NOTE THAT OUR FINAL DEADLINE IS:

4th OF AUGUST ‘Streams Inwards’ is surely not only the latest chapter of the band’s evolution but I’d say it is rather important for the whole movement. It doesn’t easily fall into any category although at the same time it’s undeniably “doom”. Where does your inspiration come from ?

It comes mainly from just what and how we feel regarding any aspect of life. We just translate that to music trying to be as honest as possible to what we find inside and outside ourselves, in order to communicate it and liberate it genuinely and fulfil a natural need of communication, in our case by means of creating and also achieving a constant feedback with the listener when playing live. In other words and due to what is said before, the idea is to let emotions decide what to show, and which topic to refer to. That is maybe also the reason why the labeling factor does not arise easily, although as you say I think from a broad perspective we actually are a Doom band.

Is there a theme or an overall construction behind the whole ‘Streams Inwards’ album? Yes. It has to be with the same source of inspiration that you asked before. The name Streams Inwards is intended to reflect the idea that emotion streams inwards and outwards every human being as the main connection and common language with everything around. I believe emotions are the most real manifestation of the self as part of a whole, a whole that is just as alive as we are, being the planet, and the universe. Man seeks to feel, whatever the kind of feeling or emotion may be. Thinking is a channel that always ends in emotion and not vice versa, although emotion indeed is what also gives birth at the same time to thought, but always “ending” on another emotion, so I think emotion is what always determines thought, irrespective of the direction and the step of the process; it is just as real as rationality is in a world where that rationality is overrated. I think rationality often works as a channel to understand the universe and everything behind, but as long as it has a name, created at the same time by its own it will always be a vicious cycle with different levels, some reached through different levels of knowledge, but a vicious cycle after all. In some way, rationality makes man believe he is something that maybe he isn't, to think he is the protagonist of a story where he maybe is no more than a secondary actor, leading to the exacerbation of this false ego that reigns and has reigned since many years. In this regard, emotion can function as the only way to go beyond, but never knowing so, just feeling it. After all this, maybe emotion is overrated too, haha but you know, equilibrium is always imperfect in order to let vibration (life, conscience) exist. But irrespective of that, “truth” lies inside emotion I think. The idea is to understand emotion as something concrete, as something real and true, just as real as empiric experience is. A common misunderstanding is to relate this conception with religion. Maybe the word religion tailors to these; it indeed does, but referring to these streams in such way, is also an attempt to eradicate that common misconception. Emotion plus rationality, equals feelings, and adjudicating emotion the same level of transcendence that is commonly given to rationality is not necessarily being religious; emotion is just as real and true as rationality is, and religion has nothing to do with these. In my case, I’m not part of any religion, but I do believe strongly that what I feel is the truth, not what I think, and I feel that my “self” is real to the extent that it is part of a whole and not vice versa. In other words, these streams inwards carry everything that we are, they give form to our inner worlds and at the same time they model our need to express, communicate and create. Music is the channel that we chose, and expressing, liberating, communicating our mostly streams inwards, has always been, at the same time, another stream outwards that models the intention/need behind our music. A question you may be tired to be asked but that is very important for us Europeans to better understand a scene so far from us and our everyday life. How’s the metal movement in Chile and Santiago in particular?

Haha no problem, although many people are aware of what happens here in that regard, there are still many people who doesn’t have an idea, and I like to let them know. In the Chilean scene we have tons of Metal bands, but people often don’t have the time or money to dedicate to a band in a more professional way. That is why it is very common that many bands stay in only a good idea and nothing else. It is a pity, because I think we have a lot of potential. The root of the problem lies in an economic factor I would say, because the potential is present in every aspect. But anyway we have a scene with good bands, bad bands, good and bad venues, respective media, support, and every component needed to have one. As for specific bands, well, to start, Poema Arcanus is great Doom band with some progressive touches, it is one of my favourite bands ever, and they are an important influence in our music. Coprofago is also a great original Chilean band, but they are not too known even in Chile; they are highly underrated here, so they hardly play live. Perpetuum is another very good band that plays a very original kind of Metal, something like Immolation meets Death and Opeth. Animus Mortis, Inanna, Wildes Heer, Target (my other band) , Torturer, Godless, Electrozombies, Uaral, Melektaus, etc are other bands that I also consider good quality... and more good bands to check out: Darkemist, Timecode, Desire of Pain, Lapsus Dei, Son in Course, Stigmatized, etc, etc. Do you think that not having the means to record and spread their music is the main hurdle for Chilean bands ?

It depends on what you mean with hurdle. Or what are the expectations of a band in terms of spreading what they do. I sometimes see bands that are happy with a good coverage in Chile and nothing else seems to matter to them speaking of spreading their music, something perfectly valid, and they sometimes manage to achieve that in many aspects not having many means. But if your goal is to reach more people, or taking your music to different type of ears, cultures, people in general, or to where the big metal market is, yes, it is indeed a hurdle. Things not easy here but I think globalization is an important factor the last 10 or 20 years that is changing increasingly fast the parameters and paradigms behind what can be seen as a hurdle. It seems something predictable, but if you compare the speed behind changes in communication over the last years everything points to big changes that are constantly evolving to unpredictability; in other words, I think anything can happen in terms of communication the next years, therefore in the means for spreading your music and everything related. You never seem completely happy about the way your albums sound. Is it the case with ‘Streams Inwards’ as well? As always, recording, mixing and producing the album was a difficult and exhausting process, although this time things happened in a more fluent way even though the schedule was very tight. Maybe that reflects on the album because every musical element on the narrative is clearer than in previous albums, although I also like that type of sound where everything is more diffuse and sometimes difficult to recognize. As for the perfect sound, well, there always details that arise with the closing out of other details; I think that’s an endless cycle that won’t ever let you be 100% satisfied, but at the same time it is the driver of an ongoing improvement. What do you want to explore - musically speaking – with Mar De Grises now and which were your targets when you started the band?

Our purpose is what I said in the first questions above. Those are still the main purposes, they have ever been. But the big difference is that with years we have just increased that purpose and incorporated other goals and purposes; it hasn’t truly changed, but augmented I would say. We started just as college mates with similar musical tastes that wanted to share with people with similar likes and points of view in life. Nowadays, that intention is still alive but we understood it in a broader and more clear way and complemented it with the goal of spreading pour music, reachingg different people, places, states, hearts and souls.
