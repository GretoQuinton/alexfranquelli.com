Nihil Novi Sub Sole - Interview

Why did you consider your experience as Deinonychus over

Interview to Nihil Novi Sub Sole Why did you consider your experience as Deinonychus over? I really appreciated the direction the music was taking and I reckon there would have been some interesting development in the near future.

When I reviewed ‘Jupiter Temple’, I wrote that the album is “an ambient nightmare, a spiral made of subtly martial, cruel, unstoppable violence” and I see this as a metaphor of what our world is nowadays. Our conditions are those of a war-less world where violence is everywhere and nowhere at the same time. Can’t man really do without violence?

Nobody likes labels (well, actually some do – but that’s their problem) but in a music shop your music would be put on the “martial ambient war-like” shelf if such a place existed.. And that would be a bad mistake. Because, on the contrary, you don’t tend to capture war in itself but more the terror derived by it. Mussolini’s words (in “Fellonia e Sangue”) are, in my opinion, not there to impress but to let the listener relive the excitement, fear and even ecstasy one could have felt the day war was declared. Am I wrong if I say that had you been in Piazza Venezia that day, you would be looking at the listeners, rather than to the dictator on the balcony?

I can hear some leaning towards contemporary and minimalist music (Tony Conrad, Morton Feldman, etc…) by means of repetitions, slow developments and other factors. Is this kind of music really an influence of yours?

Your kind of music seems to be the perfect soundtrack for a decadent age. What is your take on the situation we’re in? are global crises going to be more frequent and eventually teach us something or will we go back into our irresponsible autistic and egoistic world and live there happily forever after?

The drumming’s presence throughout the album is rather dominant. The monotony of it indeed adds a lot of emphasis to the final result. How do you compose your music and what is the instrument that you prefer when you write new stuff?

Your monicker makes one think of “disappointment”, hopes that have been betrayed. What is not new under the sun? What were your expectations when you were younger ?

When will we have a chance to see you live? What can one expect from your shows?

With Nihil Novi Sub Sole and Deinonychus combined, you have now spent more than a decade wth Italian My Kingdom Music. How did this relationship start and how has it progressed?

"We will glorify war - the world's only hygiene - militarism, patriotism, the destructive gesture of freedom-bringers, beautiful ideas worth dying for, and scorn for woman." Marinetti’s words seem to be so present in your music. These words have been misinterpreted during the years and are now undoubtedly identified with fascisms of all kinds whilst they are actually (sadly) true and we all have to come to terms with these concepts. Do you agree with Marinetti’s view?

How do you think you will develop Nihil Novi Sub Sole’s music? How and where will you push the boundaries of your music?
