Questions for Aghast Manor

by Alex Franquelli

‘Gaslight’ is a surprising album. It is made up of different layers you discover only by listening to it time after time. How do you write your music? What is the process you follow to compose tracks with such a simple but effective structure?

Just out of curiosity: is there any contemporary composer you admire? I can hear a lot of Edgard Varèse’s ‘Poeme Electronique’ for some unknown reason. What is your relationship with avant-garde?

Tell us something more about this thing with Megan Fox. What happened with her? Did she really hear ‘Das Irrlicht’ and got scared?

What fascinates me with Aghast Manor is the fact that the border between music and imagery is subtle or – sometimes – non-existing. Is there a different approach when you have to compose soundtracks?

Could you tell us something on the new Scott Derrickson’s movie? How did this collaboration come about?

I see that you’re interested in the concept of ‘shame’. Blaise Pascal used to say that ‘The only shame is to have none’. What are yours? Is there any room for shame left in such an exhibitionist society like ours?

By looking at your numerous projects (books, several music projects, fashion, a label…), you seem to have a bit of an entrepreneurial spirit. Is there something you would like to do but you haven’t had a chance to focus on yet?

Again on your several (music) outlets. Is there a scene you feel particularly attached to?

Dark ambient, folk, non-music: I am reasonably sure you do not describe your music using with none of these labels. How would you define it then?

Thanks a lot for your time ! I hope I’ll be able to see you live in London soon !

Alex Franquelli
