NDE Interview

Archive source

Full text recovered from Alex Franquelli’s author archive.

Suppose I didn't know NDE until this morning. I turned on my iPod, put my headphones on on the way to work and what I heard was spontaneously generated noise; a triumphal set of avant-gardist trains of thoughts spawned by repetition, by the obsessive use of organic loops, by saturated vocals whose intensity is not merely a medium but rather an additional tool at the service of purely nihilist matter. How many ways are there to interpret your sound ?

I could not help but “hear” some traces of minimalism here and there in the shape of (again): repetitions, drones, slow mutations, constant rhythms. Is there such an influence in your background ? Do you appreciate authors such as Michael Nyman, Morton Feldman and Tony Conrad (whose martial influence in a work such as “Outside the dream syndicate” is quite apparent, in my opinion) or is this feeling embedded in your sound by chance or by listening to more modern music ?

“War, blood, glory, ashes”. It sounds more like a manifesto rather than a mere title. What is there behind those words ? Is misanthropy the path to a self-glorifying death or is glory just a passing moment before going back to where we originally came from ?

Where do you see NDE in the over-populated extreme music scene ? How easy is it to find a balance in the realm of extremes?

Bands such Until Death Overtakes Me, Altar Of Plagues have a attempted to take a similar direction to yours through a more progressive structure: one that is both creatively extreme and structurally logic at the same time. Do you see NDE going through a similar maturation process ? If not, how do you see your music can evolve ?

More generally, do you think that the only way extreme music can evolve is through cacophony ?

Why so secretive ? Who's behind the instruments ?
